( FAQs )

Got questions? Here’s everything you
need to know about working with me.

What do you offer?

Product design, web development, brand systems, and creative direction for thoughtful digital products and experiences.

How does a project usually start?

We begin with a short discovery call to discuss your goals, scope, timing, and the best way to work together.

How long does a project take?

Most projects take four to twelve weeks, depending on their scope, complexity, and feedback cycles.

How do you price your work?

Fixed project pricing agreed before we start, based on scope rather than hours. Longer engagements can run on a monthly retainer.

Do you work with remote clients?

Yes. I’m based in Manila and work with startups and studios worldwide, with clear, async-friendly communication across time zones.
